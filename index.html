CHAS IPI.10 — Worksite protection of staff from infectionការការពារបុគ្គលិកពីការចម្លងរោគ

Classification / Weighting: Critical

This tool operationalises Measurable Element 5 of standard IPI.10. The five measurable elements are:

  1. PPE is used according to the IPC policies and procedures.
  2. Supplies for Standard and Transmission-based Precautions (gloves, gowns, mouth/eye/nose/face protection) are available and located near point of use.
  3. Healthcare workers wear gloves for procedures where contact with blood, body fluids, mucous membranes, or non-intact skin is anticipated.
  4. Gowns are properly worn to prevent contamination during procedures with anticipated contact with blood, body fluids, secretions, or excretions.
  5. There is a continuous supply of the correct kind of PPE for both clinical care and health-care waste handling and cleaning. ← tracked here

How this log supports the survey. Surveyors observe availability facility-wide and interview staff on whether PPE is routinely available. This register gives dated, point-of-use evidence that supply has been continuous, shows where the reorder level was reached, and documents that any stockout was identified and resolved.

Scoring reference · ការដាក់ពិន្ទុ

Fully met — PPE available and staff observed using it properly.

Partially met — PPE available but staff not using it properly.

Not met — PPE not available and staff not using it.

Aligned with the KCPH Standard Operating Procedure on PPE use (Technical Office). Status thresholds in this tool: Stockout = 0 in stock; Low = at or below the minimum/reorder level; Adequate = above the reorder level.
